## Speaking cards

### what

- answerZh before: 我會先寫下今天需要完成什麼，再選出截止時間最近的那件事。這樣比較容易安排順序。
- answerZh after: 我會先寫下今天需要完成什麼，然後選出截止時間最近的那件事。
- Why: 中譯多加了英文沒有的一句「這樣比較容易安排順序」，刪除以忠實對應原文。

### all

- answerEn before: When all my tasks feel urgent, I write them down and choose the first one. Seeing everything on paper makes the pressure feel less vague.
- answerEn after: When all my tasks feel urgent, I write them down and choose one to start with. Seeing everything on paper makes the pressure feel more manageable.
- answerZh before: 當所有事情都很急時，我會先把它們寫下來，再決定從哪一件開始。列出清單後，就比較能看清楚該怎麼安排。
- answerZh after: 當所有任務都感覺很急時，我會先把它們寫下來，再選一件開始做。把每件事都寫在紙上，壓力就比較容易應付。
- Why: 英文「choose the first one」語意不清、「pressure feel less vague」不自然；中譯第二句也與英文意思不符。

### when

- keywordZh before: 的時候
- keywordZh after: 當…時
- answerZh before: 我有足夠的安靜時間可以思考的時候，最像自己。沒有那段時間時，我會比較容易反應過度，也比較沒耐心。
- answerZh after: 當我有足夠安靜時間思考時，我最像自己。沒有那段時間時，我會比較容易反應過度，也比較沒耐心。
- Why: 前次修改理由錯誤：含「…」的「當…時」各部分都出現在譯文中即有效，且是 when 的標準釋義，恢復原本內容。

### fruit

- answerEn before: I would take an apple or an orange. This fruit travels well and does not need a container or refrigeration for a short outing.
- answerEn after: I would take an apple or an orange. Either fruit travels well and does not need a container or refrigeration for a short outing.
- Why: 前句提到蘋果或柳橙兩種水果，後句用單數「This fruit」指稱不正確，改為 Either fruit。

### kind

- question before: What kind action could make a newcomer feel less awkward?
- question after: What is one kind thing you could do to make a newcomer feel less awkward?
- answerEn before: A kind action would be inviting them into a small conversation. I would explain any shared references instead of leaving them outside the joke.
- answerEn after: A kind thing to do would be inviting them into a small conversation. I would explain any shared references instead of leaving them out of the joke.
- Why: 問句 What kind action 易誤讀為 what kind of；outside the joke 不自然，應為 out of。

### receipt

- answerEn after: I would keep the receipt in case I needed to return the device. Taking a photo of it would also make the purchase details easier to find.
- answerZh before: 我會留下收據，以備裝置需要退換。也拍張照片，之後比較容易找到購買資料。
- answerZh after: 我會留下收據，以備日後需要退貨。也會拍張照片，之後比較容易找到購買資料。
- Why: 「the device needed a return」不自然，第二句也沒說拍什麼；已改寫並補上 of it。

### connection

- keywordZh before: 接駁
- keywordZh after: 轉乘
- answerZh before: 我會查列車接駁需不需要換月台。如果轉乘時間太勉強，就改搭晚一班。
- answerZh after: 我會查這次轉乘需不需要換月台。如果轉乘時間太勉強，就改搭晚一班。
