# Acoustic guitar samples

These acoustic guitar notes (D2–D5, every semitone) are from the
**University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples** by Lawrence Fritts,
Electronic Music Studios, University of Iowa:
<https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html>. In its own words, the
recordings "have been freely available on this website and may be
downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

They come here by way of the edited MP3s in nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
(<https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments>, "guitar ac – Iowa"),
trimmed to three seconds and made mono for this project.
