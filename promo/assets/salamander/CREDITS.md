# Salamander Grand Piano V3

These piano samples (C2–C8, one every minor third) are from
**Salamander Grand Piano V3** by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Original: <https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3>
- These MP3s: as hosted by Tone.js, <https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/>

`scripts/make-music.py` pitch-shifts them to the notes in between, shapes
their volume and tone, and adds reverb, so the soundtrack is an adaptation
of the samples. When the videos are published, credit the piano, e.g.:

> Piano: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0)
