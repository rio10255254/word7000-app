# Marimba

From the **Versatile Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Sam Gossner and
contributors, <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL> ("Marimba", Outrigger
mallets, medium), released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

Renamed to the pitch each note actually sounds (an octave above the
library's written names, as measured), trimmed to two seconds, made mono.
