# Hand percussion and ocean drum

From the **Versatile Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Sam Gossner and
contributors, <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>, released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal**: conga, quinto and tumba open hits, high and low bongo
hits, and a sustained ocean drum (a frame drum of rolling beads that sounds
like waves), trimmed and made mono for this project.
