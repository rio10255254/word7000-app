# Baroque Soprano Recorder (sustain)

From the **Versatile Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Sam Gossner and
contributors, <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>, released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication).

The files are renamed to the pitch they actually sound (an octave above the
library's written names, as measured), trimmed, and made mono for this
project.
