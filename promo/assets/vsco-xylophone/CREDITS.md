# Xylophone samples

These xylophone notes (G4, C5, G5, C6, G6, C7, G7) are from the
**Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition** (VSCO 2 CE),
<http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html>, which is "Licensed under
CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) — you can do whatever you want with these
samples."

They come here by way of the edited MP3s in nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
(<https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments>, "xylophone – VSO2"),
trimmed to two seconds and made mono for this project.
