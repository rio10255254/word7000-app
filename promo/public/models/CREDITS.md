# Phone model

`iphone-15-pro-max.glb`: "Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max Black" by polyman, from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/apple-iphone-15-pro-max-black-df17520841214c1792fb8a44c6783ee7), [CC BY 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Tinted white and its display replaced with real App screenshots in the 3D countdown reels. Credit the author wherever the reels' credits are listed.

`../draco/`: the Draco mesh decoder that ships with three.js (Apache 2.0), needed to read the model.
