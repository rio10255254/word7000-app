# Phone model

`iphone-16/`: "iPhone 16 - Free" by wimell, from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/iphone-16-free-d58591e88a824dfd8cef0af616273b02), Sketchfab Free Standard licence. Its textures are left out, and every part is coloured by hand as the white model (matte white glass back, glossier white camera pill, silver aluminium band and keys, polished logo), and its display is replaced with real App screenshots in the 3D countdown reels.
