# Phone model

`iphone-16/`: "iPhone 16 - Free" by wimell, from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/iphone-16-free-d58591e88a824dfd8cef0af616273b02), Sketchfab Free Standard licence. Its textures are left out, so the back glass and the logo are coloured by hand to match iPhone 16 Teal, and its display is replaced with real App screenshots in the 3D countdown reels.
