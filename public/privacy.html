單字貓 WordCat 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

中文English

簡短版

  • 不登入（訪客）也能背單字，但進度不會保存，也不會上傳。
  • 用 Apple 登入後，學習進度會同步到我們的伺服器，換手機也找得回來。
  • 錄音只留在你的 iPhone，從不上傳；離開練習就刪除。
  • 線上 AI 只在你同意後使用，只傳送當次需要的文字，以及掃描時的單字表照片；不傳錄音、姓名或 Email。
  • 我們不賣資料、不放廣告、不做追蹤或第三方分析。
  • 你可以隨時在 App 裡永久刪除帳號和所有資料。

帳號與登入

第一次打開 App 時會建立一個臨時的訪客身分。訪客可以學習，但學習進度不會保存，關閉 App 後就不見了。

選擇「用 Apple 登入」時，我們會收到 Apple 提供的帳號識別碼，以及你同意分享的 Email（可以是 Apple 的轉寄地址），只用來連結並還原你的學習資料。為了在你刪除帳號時，能向 Apple 撤銷這次登入授權，我們會把 Apple 提供的授權權杖保存在後台不對外開放的資料表；刪除帳號時會先向 Apple 撤銷，再把它刪除。

我們保存的學習資料

登入後，我們會保存並同步：單字學習進度與複習狀態、收藏的單字、自訂詞庫（包括掃描建立的詞庫）、顯示與練習偏好，以及每日學習紀錄（連續天數與練習次數）。這些資料只用來提供 App 的功能。

你的 iPhone 上也會保存一份副本，讓 App 不用網路也能使用。

每次打開 App，會回報目前的 App 版本、iOS 版本、手機型號（例如 iPhone 17）、介面語言和最後開啟時間，以及 Apple 為單字貓這個 App 產生的裝置識別碼（只能用在單字貓、不能跨 App 追蹤，用來分辨同一個帳號的不同手機；每支手機只保留最新一筆）。這些資料讓我們知道還有多少人在用舊版本、新版在哪些機型上要特別測試，並能只對舊版本發送更新提醒；刪除帳號時一起刪除。

我們也會統計各功能的使用次數，例如每天學了幾張卡、做了幾次口說練習、用了幾次 AI、是否打開付費頁或完成購買，以及選用的字庫與級數。這些統計只有「功能名稱和次數」，不包含單字內容、答案、錄音、照片、姓名或 Email，只用來了解哪些功能有人用、決定接下來改善什麼。統計資料跟你的帳號（或訪客身分）連在一起，最多保留 400 天，刪除帳號時一起刪除；不會用於廣告或追蹤，也不會提供給第三方。

錄音與語音辨識

只有在你按下錄音時，App 才會請求麥克風權限。每段錄音最長 60 秒，存在 iPhone 受保護的暫存空間，不會上傳；離開練習就會刪除，沒刪掉的暫存檔也會在下次開啟 App 時清除。

把錄音轉成文字使用 iPhone 內建的離線語音辨識。第一次使用時，iPhone 可能會自動從 Apple 下載該語言的辨識資源；錄音本身不會被送出去辨識。系統鍵盤的聽寫功能則依 Apple 的設定處理。

掃描單字表

掃描時拍的照片或選的圖片，若你同意使用線上 AI（Premium），會傳送到 Anthropic 的 Claude 讀出單字與意思，我們的伺服器不保存照片；沒有同意、沒有網路或線上 AI 無法使用時，照片在 iPhone 上辨識，不會上傳。

AI 功能

口說批改、單字解說與提問、測驗解說、掃描整理、單字補完和學習分析會使用 AI。

  • 手機上的 AI：支援 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone，可以用 Apple 的裝置端模型，內容不會離開你的手機（若 Apple 交給其 Private Cloud Compute 處理，Apple 表示不會保存）。
  • 線上 AI（Premium）：訂閱 Premium 並在 App 中同意後，會使用 Anthropic, PBC（美國）提供的 Claude。只會送出該次請求需要的內容：你的回答與題目、掃描的單字表照片（或辨識出的文字）、你查詢的單字和你打的問題，或在你要求學習分析時，最近 14 天的摘要（練習次數、測驗正確率、連續天數，以及最多 12 個最常忘記的單字）。錄音、其他照片、姓名、Email 和完整學習紀錄都不會送出。
  • 請求會先經過我們的伺服器，確認你已登入並訂閱 Premium，並計算每個帳號每天的使用次數以公平分配；我們不保存請求內容。Anthropic 依其商業資料條款處理文字並回傳建議。
  • 你可以隨時在「設定 → AI 與錄音」拒絕或關閉線上 AI。AI 解說會存在你的 iPhone，方便再次打開，不會上傳到帳號。AI 的建議可能有錯。

訂閱（Premium）

訂閱透過 Apple 的 App Store 購買與付款，我們拿不到你的付款資料。為了確認 Premium 狀態，我們會保存 Apple 提供的訂閱交易識別碼，以及它對應的帳號。Apple 也會把續訂、取消與退款等通知傳給我們的伺服器，用來更新 Premium 狀態。

通知

如果你允許通知，我們會保存 Apple 提供的裝置推播權杖、介面語言、App 版本，以及帳號類型（訪客、免費或 Premium），用來發送 App 公告。如果你開啟鎖定畫面連續學習提醒，我們也會保存你的連續天數截止時間與時區，只用來在連續紀錄快中斷時啟動倒數。你可以隨時在 iPhone 設定中關閉通知。

我們不做的事

我們不販售或出租個人資料、沒有廣告、不跨 App 或網站追蹤你，也不使用第三方分析工具。App 可能會讀取一個公開的功能設定檔，不附帶帳號、錄音或回答；網路服務商會依正常運作收到連線資訊。

資料保存與刪除

你可以在 App 的「我」頁進入帳號，選「刪除帳號與資料」永久刪除。刪除後會移除學習進度、自訂詞庫、每日紀錄、通知與提醒登記、AI 使用次數、功能使用統計、訂閱對應紀錄，並向 Apple 撤銷登入授權（刪除前會請你用 Apple 確認一次）。訂閱本身要在 iPhone 的「設定 → Apple ID → 訂閱」取消，刪除帳號不會自動取消訂閱。

兒童

本 App 不會在知情的情況下收集 13 歲以下兒童的個人資料。如果你認為孩子提供了個人資料，請聯絡我們刪除。

聯絡我們

有任何隱私問題，請寄信到 rio10255254@gmail.com，或到支援頁面。若政策有重大變更，會更新本頁的日期並在 App 中告知。

WordCat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In short

  • You can study without signing in, but as a guest your progress is not saved or uploaded.
  • Signed in with Apple, your progress syncs to our server so it comes back on another iPhone.
  • Recordings stay on your iPhone, are never uploaded, and are deleted when you leave the practice.
  • Online AI is used only after you agree, and only what a request needs is sent (text, and the photo of a word list you scan): never recordings, your name or email.
  • No selling of data, no ads, no tracking or third-party analytics.
  • You can permanently delete your account and data in the app at any time.

Account and sign-in

When the app first opens it creates a temporary guest identity. Guests can study, but their progress is not saved and is gone when the app closes.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive Apple's account identifier and, if you share it, your email (which may be an Apple relay address), used only to connect and restore your learning data. So that deleting your account can also revoke this sign-in with Apple, we keep the authorization token Apple issues in a private table on our server that the app cannot read; on deletion we revoke it with Apple and then delete it.

Learning data we keep

When you are signed in we store and sync: word progress and review status, saved words, your own word lists (including scanned ones), display and practice preferences, and daily activity (streak and practice counts). This data is used only to provide the app. A copy is also kept on your iPhone so the app works offline.

Each time the app opens it reports its version, the iOS version, the iPhone model (for example iPhone 17), the interface language and when it was last opened, with the device identifier Apple gives WordCat for that iPhone (it works for WordCat only and cannot track you across apps; it tells one account’s phones apart, and only the latest report per phone is kept). This tells us how many people still run old versions and which models to test new versions on, and lets us remind only them to update. It is deleted with your account.

We also count how often each feature is used: for example how many cards you study a day, how many speaking practices you do, how often you use AI, whether you open the subscription page or complete a purchase, and which word list and levels you choose. These counts contain only feature names and numbers, never your words, answers, recordings, photos, name or email, and are used only to learn which features people use and what to improve next. They are linked to your account (or guest identity), kept for at most 400 days and deleted with your account; they are never used for ads or tracking or shared with third parties.

Recordings and speech recognition

The app asks for the microphone only when you tap record. Each recording is at most 60 seconds, kept in protected temporary storage on your iPhone and never uploaded; leaving the practice deletes it, and any left-over temporary file is removed the next time the app opens.

Turning a recording into text uses the iPhone's on-device speech recognition. The first time, your iPhone may download that language's recognition assets from Apple automatically; the recording itself is not sent anywhere for recognition. System keyboard dictation follows Apple's settings.

Scanning word lists

If you agreed to online AI (Premium), photos you take or pick for scanning are sent to Anthropic's Claude to read the words and meanings; our server does not store them. Without that agreement, offline, or when online AI is unavailable, photos are read on your iPhone and are not uploaded.

AI features

The speaking coach, word explanations and questions, quiz explanations, scan tidy-up, word fill-in and learning insights use AI.

  • On-device AI: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model can be used and the content stays on your iPhone (when Apple hands a request to its Private Cloud Compute, Apple says it is not stored).
  • Online AI (Premium): with a Premium subscription and once you agree in the app, Claude by Anthropic, PBC (United States) is used. Only what a request needs is sent: your answer and its question, the photo of a word list you scan (or the text recognised from it), the word you look up and any question you type, or, when you ask for learning insights, a 14-day summary (practice counts, quiz accuracy, streak and up to 12 words you forget most). Recordings, other photos, your name, email and full learning history are never sent.
  • Requests pass through our server, which checks that you are signed in and subscribed and counts requests per account per day to share the service fairly; it does not store their content. Anthropic processes the text to return a suggestion under its commercial data terms.
  • You can decline or turn off online AI at any time in Settings → AI and recording. AI explanations are kept on your iPhone so they reopen quickly; they are not uploaded to your account. AI suggestions may contain mistakes.

Subscriptions (Premium)

Subscriptions are bought and paid for through Apple's App Store; we never receive your payment details. To confirm Premium we keep the subscription's transaction identifier from Apple and the account it belongs to. Apple also sends our server notices about renewals, cancellations and refunds, which we use to keep Premium status current.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we keep the device push token from Apple, your interface language, the app version and your account type (guest, free or Premium) to send app announcements. If you turn on Lock Screen streak reminders, we also keep your streak deadline and time zone, used only to start the countdown when your streak is about to end. You can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings at any time.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent personal data, show ads, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics. The app may fetch a small public feature-settings file without account identifiers, recordings or answers; hosting providers necessarily receive ordinary connection information.

Keeping and deleting data

You can permanently delete your account in the app: on the Me tab, open your account and choose Delete account and data. This removes your learning progress, word lists, daily records, notification and reminder registrations, AI usage counts, feature usage counts and subscription links, and revokes the sign-in with Apple (you confirm with Apple once before deleting). A subscription itself is cancelled in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; deleting the account does not cancel it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
