Word7000 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What Word7000 stores

Word7000 stores vocabulary progress, saved words, review status, custom word sets, display preferences, and basic daily activity records used to keep learning progress in sync.

Account sign-in

Word7000 creates a temporary guest identity when the app first opens. If you later sign in with Google or Apple, Word7000 uses your account identifier and email only to connect and restore your learning data.

Local data

Display preferences and a local copy of learning progress are stored on your device. Removing the app or clearing its storage may remove the connection to a temporary guest identity.

Cloud sync

Guest progress may be backed up to the temporary identity used on that device. Signing in is required for custom sets, data tools, and reliable restoration on another device.

Lock Screen streak reminders

If you enable Lock Screen streak reminders, Word7000 stores an Apple device token, your streak deadline, interface language, and time zone. This data is used only to start the countdown you requested when your streak is close to expiring.

Optional speaking recordings and on-device suggestions

In versions that include recording practice, microphone access is requested only when you choose to record. Each recording is limited to 60 seconds and is kept in protected temporary storage on your device, not uploaded. Leaving the recording screen deletes it; abandoned temporary recordings are removed when the app next launches. Answers and suggestions remain only in the current practice session and are not added to account sync.

Transcription uses on-device recognition only when supported for your device and language. You may choose to download language assets from Apple; the app does not upload your recording for recognition. System keyboard dictation is separate and follows Apple's settings.

Online AI (Anthropic's Claude). The speaking coach, word explanations, quiz explanations, scan tidy-up, word fill-in suggestions and learning insights use Claude, an online AI model provided by Anthropic, PBC (United States), once you agree in the app; you can decline or turn this off at any time in AI and recording, and the app then uses Apple's on-device model where available. When online AI is used, only the text needed for that request is sent: your typed or transcribed answer and its practice question, the text recognised from a scanned word list, the word you ask about, or, when you ask for learning insights, a summary of the last 14 days (practice counts, quiz accuracy, streak and up to 12 words you forget most often). Recordings, photos, your name, email address and your full learning history are not sent. Requests pass through our own account server, which checks that you are signed in (a guest account is enough) and counts requests per account per day to share the service fairly; it does not store their content. Anthropic processes the text to return a suggestion under its commercial data terms. If you agreed to the earlier Groq-based online AI, the app asks again before sending anything to Anthropic. AI suggestions may contain mistakes.

Apart from the daily request count above, the app does not collect AI capability, answer, or satisfaction statistics. It may fetch a small public feature-control file, without including account identifiers, recordings, or answers. Hosting providers necessarily receive ordinary network connection information for that request. Existing sign-in, learning sync, and reminder data are handled separately as described above.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete the current Word7000 identity and its data from the app settings. This removes learning progress, custom sets, daily activity records, and Lock Screen reminder registrations connected to that identity.
