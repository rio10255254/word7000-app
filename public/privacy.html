Word7000 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

What Word7000 stores

Word7000 stores vocabulary progress, saved words, review status, custom word sets, display preferences, and basic daily activity records used to keep learning progress in sync.

Account sign-in

If you sign in with Google or Apple, authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Word7000 uses your account identifier and email only to connect your learning data to your account.

Local data

When you use the app without signing in, learning data is stored on your device. Removing the app or clearing browser/app storage may remove local-only data.

Cloud sync

When signed in, learning progress and custom sets may be saved to Supabase so they can be restored on another device signed in to the same account.

Data deletion

You can delete your Word7000 cloud learning data from the app settings. This removes synced learning progress, custom sets, and daily activity rows connected to your signed-in account.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the support page: Word7000 Support.
