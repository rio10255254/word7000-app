單字貓是什麼

單字貓（WordCat）是 iPhone 上的英文單字 App，收錄 7000 個常用單字，提供每日學習、複習、測驗、掃描單字表和口說練習。

恢復購買

換手機或重新安裝後，打開 Premium 升級畫面，按「恢復購買」，並使用當初付款的 Apple 帳號。

取消訂閱

在 App 的「我的」頁按「管理訂閱」，或到 iPhone 的「設定 → Apple 帳號 → 訂閱項目 → 單字貓」取消。取消後，已付費的期間結束前仍可使用 Premium。刪除帳號不會自動取消訂閱。

刪除帳號與資料

在 App 的「我的 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號與資料」，確認後會永久刪除這個帳號的學習進度、字庫和每日紀錄，無法復原。

What WordCat is

WordCat is an iPhone app for learning 7000 common English words, with daily study, review, quizzes, word-list scanning and speaking practice.

Restore purchases

After switching phones or reinstalling, open the Premium upgrade screen and tap Restore, using the Apple Account you paid with.

Cancel the subscription

In the app, go to Me and tap Manage subscription, or on your iPhone go to Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → WordCat. Premium stays available until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Me → Account → Delete account and data. This permanently deletes the account's progress, word banks and daily records, and cannot be undone.
