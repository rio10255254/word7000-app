Word7000 Support

Need help with Word7000? This page covers sign-in, sync, subscriptions, updates, and account data requests.

Common checks

  • If sign-in does not return to the app, close the sign-in page and try again from Word7000.
  • If progress does not sync, sign out and sign in again with the same Apple, Google, or email account.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, choose Use email, enter your address and tap Forgot password. Open the email on your iPhone so its link returns to Word7000, then choose a new password.
  • Online AI (Claude) needs a signed-in account. Guests use the AI on their iPhone instead.
  • If scanning shows a message about the camera, turn on Camera for Word7000 in the iPhone's Settings, or choose a photo instead.
  • If a web app update does not appear, use the update prompt or refresh the page.

Word7000 Plus subscriptions

  • Plus is billed by Apple through your Apple Account. To cancel, open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Word7000. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.
  • New phone or reinstalled? Open the Plus page in Word7000 and tap Restore.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account data

You can permanently delete the current Word7000 identity and its stored data from the app's settings screen. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Settings as described above.
